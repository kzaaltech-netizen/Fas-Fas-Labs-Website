# 03 — Brand and Voice

## Positioning

FAS FAS Labs is an independent product studio from India. A small team builds consumer products end to end: idea, design, code, growth. Three products are live today, across three very different categories:

| Product | Category | Who it is for |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| Jyotishly | Astrology / belief | People who follow Vedic astrology: kundli, daily horoscope, panchang |
| PlacementQuest | Careers / learning | Students and freshers preparing for company placement tests |
| ArrowTheGame | Games / play | Casual puzzle players who like quick rounds and 1v1 battles |

The range is the point: one team, one shared core, shipping into very different markets.

## Tagline options

Pick one. Recommended first.

1. **"Fast to ship. Built to stay."** Speed (the name) plus quality (why people return). Works as hero and closing CTA.
2. "Small team. Products people open daily."
3. "We build the apps we'd use ourselves."

Hero headline under the tagline: **"We build consumer products people come back to."**

## Voice

- **Short and plain.** Short sentences. Specific nouns. No "solutions", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "transform your business".
- **Confident, not loud.** State what we built. Let the products prove it.
- **Honest numbers.** Only real figures. No rounding up into vanity.
- **A little playful.** We make a game and an astrology app; the site can smile. One light line per section, not more.
- **Indian and global.** Built in India, written in plain international English.

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "PlacementQuest gives you company-wise aptitude practice with worked answers." | "PlacementQuest is a revolutionary AI-powered career solution." |
| "Three products live. More in the lab." | "A growing ecosystem of world-class digital experiences." |
| "We ship every week." | "We are passionate about innovation." |

## Visual identity

- **Mark:** two copper plates forming a stylised double F. Upper plate a light copper, lower plate darker bronze.
- **Motif uses:** hero assembly animation; a small two-plate chevron as list bullets and section markers; plate-shaped clip path on product card hover; giant "FAS FAS" wordmark in the footer.
- **Color mood:** warm. Warm black and warm cream, never cold grey. Copper as the only brand accent. Each product brings its own accent inside its own showcase only.
- **Type mood:** bold grotesque display with an italic serif for one or two emphasis words per headline (codeway-style).
- **Imagery:** real product screenshots in clean device frames. No stock photos, no robots, no abstract 3D blobs.

## Naming rules

- Company: **FAS FAS Labs** (all caps FAS FAS, title-case Labs). Short form "FAS FAS".
- Products: **Jyotishly**, **PlacementQuest**, **ArrowTheGame** (one word, camel case as on their sites).
