BRAND VOICE
----------------------------------------
- Calm, confident, minimal — short declarative sentences ("Transform Your Home," "Laugh and Learn," "Grows With You").
- Warm and human-centric rather than technical-first; leads with lifestyle benefit before spec/feature.
- Aspirational and mission-driven ("truly abundant society," "do more of what we love").
- Friendly, almost domestic language when describing the robot itself (NEO is called "friendly," "cozy," "gentle," a "companion").
- Uses simple two-to-three word section headers (Utility, Design, Companion, Intelligence) followed by punchy benefit statements.
- Product/spec language becomes more technical and precise only in the deep-dive spec sheet (hardware tables), contrasting with the soft emotional tone of the marketing copy.

ADDITIONAL NOTES
----------------------------------------
Company background (from About page):
- Originally founded in 2014 as Halodi Robotics by CEO Bernt Øyvind Børnich
- 2018: Developed Revo1, described as the world's highest torque-to-weight-ratio drive servo motor, built for low gear-ratio robotics
- 2022: EVE (wheeled industrial humanoid) deployed into customer factories globally
- 2023: Pivoted focus toward the home with NEO
- 2024: Introduced NEO Beta publicly
- 2025: NEO Gamma introduced; private early access program begins welcoming general public
- 2026: NEO launches into Early Access customer homes; US deliveries begin

NEO Hardware Specs (for reference/spec-sheet content):
- Height: 5'6" | Weight: 66 lbs | Lift: 154 lbs | Carry: 55 lbs | Arm Payload: 18 lbs
- Degrees of Freedom: Hands 22x2, Arms 7x2, Neck 3, Spine 2, Legs 6x2
- Speed: Hands 8.0 m/s, Max run speed 6.2 m/s, Walking speed 1.4 m/s
- Battery: 842 Wh capacity, 4h run-time, 6 min quick charge per hour of runtime
- Safety: Soft body with custom lattice polymer, no pinch points, HIC <250, low-inertia tendon drives
- Ingress Protection: Hands IP68 (submersible), Body IP44 (splash proof)
- Compute: 1X NEO Cortex (Nvidia Jetson Thor), up to 2070 FP4 TFLOPS, 4 beamforming microphones, 3-stage speakers, dual 8.85MP 90Hz stereo fisheye cameras, Wifi/Bluetooth/5G
